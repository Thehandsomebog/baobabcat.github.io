Custom AI Solutions

Build a tailored AI-enabled workflow when the business problem does not fit neatly into a generic product category.

Who it is for

Organizations with proprietary workflows, internal knowledge systems, or edge cases that off-the-shelf tooling cannot handle cleanly.

Business outcomes

A workflow that fits the business, a cleaner team experience, and less compromise around tool limitations.

How the work starts

We define the smallest valuable workflow that standard software cannot support, then document its data, permissions, review points, integrations, and success measures before choosing a technical approach.

Examples

  • internal copilots
  • knowledge retrieval systems
  • specialized review and drafting workflows
  • team-specific operational assistants

When not to use this

If the problem is a straightforward support queue, automation flow, or training need, the more specific service pages may be a better fit than commissioning a custom system first.

What to scope together

A workflow-specific prototype, evaluation criteria, and a defined path from testing to an owned operating system. The final deliverables are agreed around your existing tools, data access, and operating constraints.

What your team brings

A workflow owner, representative examples that are safe to review, and time with the people responsible for approvals and day-to-day use.

Boundaries and timing

Start with one defined workflow. Integrations, ongoing support, rollout scope, and timing are agreed before work begins; they are not assumed to be unlimited. Bring access or approval dependencies into the first conversation.
