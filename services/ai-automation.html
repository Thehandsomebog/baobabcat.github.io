AI Workflow Automation

Automate repeatable work across intake, triage, document handling, and internal handoffs without making the workflow harder to supervise.

Who it is for

Teams buried in repetitive work, queue management, and manual coordination across functions.

Business outcomes

Time recovered, cleaner handoffs, and more time for specialists to focus on work that needs their judgment.

How the work starts

We map one repeatable workflow from trigger to completion, including its exceptions, approvals, source systems, and handoffs. That creates a measurable automation target instead of a vague tool project.

Typical automation targets

  • email or support triage
  • document extraction and routing
  • lead qualification and intake
  • status updates and internal handoffs

What makes it work

The workflow has to be redesigned around the automation. BaobabCat builds the decision path, exception handling, and human review layer so the automation does not become a new source of noise.

What to scope together

A mapped trigger-to-handoff workflow, explicit exception rules, and a testable automation with human review points. The final deliverables are agreed around your existing tools, data access, and operating constraints.

What your team brings

A workflow owner, representative examples that are safe to review, and time with the people responsible for approvals and day-to-day use.

Boundaries and timing

Start with one defined workflow. Integrations, ongoing support, rollout scope, and timing are agreed before work begins; they are not assumed to be unlimited. Bring access or approval dependencies into the first conversation.
